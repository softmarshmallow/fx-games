# The Lantern Ferry: universe expansion direction

Expand the synopsis and the poster into one small, coherent, explorable
universe for a reader who has never seen either. This package is a committed
fixture, so the world is meant to stay cheap to hold in the head: prefer a
subject that reveals a rule, a cost, a trade, or a wound over a subject that
only adds a name.

The poster is visual evidence and art grammar only. Its dusk palette, its
banded flat light, its hard silhouettes, and its single amber source describe
how this world is drawn. They state nothing about what is true in it. Read the
water, the hung lantern, the moored hull, and the landing posts as staging, and
take every world fact from the synopsis.

The direction below is rationale, never evidence. Cite a requirement id to
justify a generated extension or to show direction coverage; never record one
as a fact about the world.

## Requirements

- direction_cold_reader: Every entity summary and fact must stand on its own
  for a reader who has not seen the synopsis. Explain flakeshell, lampreed, the
  bloom, the reading, and the charter the first time each one matters.
- direction_irregular_census: Choose an irregular census between the package
  minimum and maximum. Reach all eight classes, but do not pad a class to
  balance the table; a sparse class is correct when the world is this small.
- direction_visible_mechanics: Every rule this world runs on must be
  stageable. The bloom, the amber-and-green channel reading, the spring fords,
  the winter slabs, the shore rope, the oil press, and the toll must each show
  up as something a person does or a surface shows, never as an abstraction
  stated in text alone.
- direction_physical_river: The Sennow stays a river and the flakeshell stays
  an animal. No divinity, spirits, prophecy, spellcasting, speaking water, or
  light that burns without fuel. Every advantage in this world costs oil,
  hours, or risk, and the costs must stay named.
- direction_legitimate_disagreement: Both banks keep a real material stake.
  Neither the Landing Ferry Company's charter nor the Kestwick Lamp
  Subscription may be written as merely greedy or merely righteous, and the
  marsh cutters are a third position, not a chorus. Name what each side loses
  if the other wins.
- direction_extension_layer: One clearly lineaged layer of plausible extension
  is allowed: crossings and works upstream, earlier bloom years, the tools of
  the trade, marsh custom, and the wording of the charter. Mark every such
  claim as a generated extension and cite this requirement as its rationale.
- direction_poster_grammar_only: A detail visible only on the poster, such as
  the high hung lantern, the raked stern post, or the pair of mooring posts,
  may enter the package only as a generated extension citing this requirement
  and a poster observation. It is never recorded as synopsis canon.
- direction_one_image_one_lesson: Each concept image must teach one thing no
  other image in the set teaches. Two images must not both explain the channel
  reading, the same trade, or the same institution. Vary distance, hour, and
  whether the frame is at work or at rest; darkness is the working condition
  here, so a night scene must still earn its subject.
- direction_no_explanatory_images: Produce no diagram, map, chart, timeline,
  cutaway, blueprint, atlas page, turnaround, variant grid, sheet, kit,
  standalone emblem, or infographic. Topology, chronology, causality, and rules
  stay in text and structured data.
